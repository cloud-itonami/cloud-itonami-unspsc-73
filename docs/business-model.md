# Business Model: Independent Industrial Cleaning & Certification Robotics

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-unspsc-73`
- UNSPSC segment: `73` (Industrial Production and Manufacturing Services)
- Activity: industrial equipment/facility cleaning with governed
  post-clean certification
- Social impact: safer facilities, verified contamination control, local
  contractor jobs

## Customer

- manufacturing plants needing scheduled equipment cleaning
- food/pharma facilities requiring certified contamination-free records
- facility managers wanting a governed, auditable cleaning contractor
  instead of an unaudited crew

## Offer

- scheduled equipment/facility cleaning
- post-clean sensor certification (residue, contamination scan)
- certification record with sensor evidence
- confined-space or hazardous-residue escalation (human sign-off, never
  automated)
- compliance and certification-history audit export

## Revenue

- per-job cleaning + certification fee
- facility subscription (recurring cleaning contract)
- compliance certification package for regulated industries (food/pharma)
- audit-export package for customer's own compliance records

## Trust Controls

- certify/re-clean recommendations require Industrial Cleaning Governor
  clearance
- any `:high`/`:safety-critical` finding (confined space, hazardous
  residue) always escalates to human sign-off
- every clean, finding and escalation is logged
- public contamination/certification claims must reference measured
  sensor data
